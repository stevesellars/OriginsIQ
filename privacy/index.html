Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 01, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OriginsIQ LLC ("OriginsIQ," "we," "us") handles personal information in connection with our products, such as ExcursionsIQ (each, a "Service"), our advisory and custom AI services, and our corporate website at originsiq.com. Our website only collects the details you send us when you get in touch; the information our customers use our products for is held in the products themselves.

1. Our two roles

When we act for our customers. In legal terms, we are their "processor" or "service provider": we hold their information for them, and they decide how it is used. Our customers are businesses in the travel industry, such as shore excursion operators and brokers (each, an "Organization"). When an Organization uses a Service, the information it enters — about its staff, guides, tour staff, vendors, port agents and operations — belongs to that Organization. We process it on the Organization's behalf and under its instructions, as set out in our agreement with it. If you are a guide, staff member or contact of an Organization and have a question about your information, please contact that Organization first; we will help it respond.

When we decide how information is used. In legal terms, we are the "controller" of this information. We are responsible for information about people who visit our website, contact us, request a demo, engage us for advisory or custom AI services, or manage an Organization's account with us (for example, billing and contract contacts), and for the security records we keep to protect our Services (such as sign-in events, records of administrator access and security alerts). Information a client shares with us during an advisory engagement is handled under that engagement's agreement.

2. Information our products don't collect

Some products have extra limits on what may be entered. For example, ExcursionsIQ works with guest counts, not guest identities. It is not designed to hold cruise passengers' or tour guests' names, contact details, cabin or booking numbers, or ID details, and our customers agree not to enter them. If we find that such information has been entered, we may ask the Organization to remove it, or delete it ourselves.

4. How we use it

  • To provide the Services our customers have asked for — running their operations workspace, portals, notifications and AI features. (Legal basis: our contract with the Organization, and its instructions.)

  • To let our AI assistants learn for your Organization, by keeping their memory of your Organization's questions, answers and documents in your Organization's workspace. Something one user discusses with an AI assistant may help answer a colleague's question, subject to their access level. It is never used for another Organization. (Legal basis: our contract with the Organization.)

  • To secure the Services — authentication, preventing and investigating misuse, audit records, security monitoring (including AI-assisted analysis of security events, reviewed by our staff), and keeping each Organization's data separate. (Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the Services secure — a reason the law accepts when it doesn't override your rights — and legal obligations.)

  • To support you, including investigating support requests (our staff may use AI to help, and review its output), and to send service and security notices. (Legal basis: contract; legitimate interests.)

  • To improve our Services and understand platform trends, using usage data and aggregated, de-identified information that does not identify any person, Organization or cruise line. (Legal basis: legitimate interests.)

  • To deliver advisory services, respond to enquiries and bill, and to keep business records. (Legal basis: contract; legitimate interests; legal obligations.)

  • To comply with law and enforce our agreements.

We do not sell personal information, we do not share it to target advertising, and our AI providers do not use it to train their models.

5. Who we share it with

  • Service providers (sometimes called "subprocessors") who host and operate our Services under contracts that require them to protect the information and use it only to serve us. The current list for each product is available at originsiq.com/subprocessors.

  • Your Organization, whose administrators can see and manage information in their workspace.

  • People your Organization chooses to share with, for example through a share link to a schedule or a link with another organization.

  • Recipients you choose to message through a Service (for example, a guide receiving a dispatch).

  • Legal and safety, where required by law or court order, or to protect rights, property or safety.

  • Business transfers, to a successor in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

6. AI providers

Our AI features send the minimum context needed to answer a request to AI model providers who work for us as service providers. Before a request leaves our systems, credential data such as password hashes and authentication secrets is removed. We have opted out of model training with each provider, and under their current terms they do not use the data to train their models. Providers may keep request data for a short period, generally up to 30 days, to operate their service and detect abuse.

7. Where information is stored

Our Services are hosted in the United States. If you access them from The Bahamas, the Caribbean or elsewhere, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States. We protect it as described here wherever it is processed.

8. How long we keep it

  • Organization workspace data: for the life of the Organization's agreement, then 30 days for export, then deleted within a further 30 days. Backups expire on their normal cycle.

  • AI assistant memory: each product's AI assistant uses memory for a set period and then stops using it; all of it is deleted with the Organization's workspace data. Organizations can ask us to set a shorter period. In ExcursionsIQ, Eve uses conversation memory for 90 days (Starter plan), 365 days (Pro plan) or as configured (Enterprise plan), and keeps weekly operational summaries for the life of the Organization's agreement.

  • Audit and security records: certain sign-in events, 90 days; certain routine operational events, 30 days; security detections, 90 days; certain changes to users, settings and features, 365 days. Other audit records — including records of administrator access and data exports — are kept for the life of the Organization's account and deleted with its workspace data.

  • Business contact, billing and contract records: as long as needed for the relationship and as required by tax and accounting law.

  • Website enquiries: up to 24 months after last contact.

9. Security

We use measures designed to protect personal information, including encryption in transit, hashed passwords and sign-in codes, limits on repeated sign-in attempts, role-based access, keeping each Organization's data to that Organization, audit records, and restricted, time-limited and logged administrator access. Our staff access an Organization's workspace only at its request (for example, for support or onboarding) or to deal with a security threat or legal requirement. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects information we hold for an Organization, we will notify the Organization, which is responsible for telling you or regulators where the law requires. If it affects information we control, we will notify you and regulators as the law requires.

10. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (including under The Bahamas' Data Protection (Privacy of Personal Information) Act 2003 and similar laws), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to receive a copy of it.

  • If your information is in an Organization's workspace, contact that Organization; we'll assist it.

  • If you receive WhatsApp messages through one of our products, you can turn them off in your portal settings where available, or by asking the Organization that sends them.

  • Otherwise, email support@originsiq.com. We'll verify your request and respond within 30 days, or the period the law requires. We won't treat you any differently for using these rights.

11. Cookies

We use only cookies needed to run our Services: to keep you signed in (including to product portals, such as ExcursionsIQ's guide and tour-staff portals), and to remember your language, display theme, and the filters and tabs you last used. We do not use advertising or analytics tracking cookies in our Services. We use error-monitoring software (Sentry) to detect and fix problems; it may record technical details of an error and your browser.

12. Children

Our Services are for business use by adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information about anyone under 18, and users must be 18 or older.

13. Changes

We'll post updates here and change the "Last updated" date. For material changes, we'll notify Organizations and users in the app or by email.
